GUIDE

Introduction

mOmentiOn connects your riding speed to your action camera. Set your recording thresholds before riding, then review the route afterward.

Automatic Recording

Recording starts and stops when your configured GPS speed and delay conditions are met. Manual recording is also supported.

Camera Compatibility

Insta360 and DJI connection settings are available. Supported operations depend on camera model, firmware, and platform. Verify your equipment before riding.

Before You Ride

Configure and test while stationary. Do not operate your phone while riding. GPS reception and camera connection affect automatic recording.
