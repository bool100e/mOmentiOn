GUIDE

Getting Started

1. Permissions & Storage

Follow the permission setup and select a storage folder where offered.

2. Connect Your Camera

Select your camera brand in Settings and verify its connection details. Enable Bluetooth and Wi-Fi, then check the camera indicator.

3. Set Recording Thresholds

Choose start and stop speeds and delays. Check GPS reception and test manual recording while stationary before departure.

4. Review Your Route

After riding, open Saved rides to replay, compare, and export. Open Saved logs to investigate connection problems.
