mOmentiOn - Action Cam Remote
Auto Record by Speed & GPS Log

App description

Control compatible Insta360 360 and DJI Osmo cameras over Bluetooth. Start and stop recording at your selected speeds, or use manual remote control.

Background GPS, GPX import and export, speed heatmaps, speed and estimated lean angle and elevation graphs, ride comparisons, and image and video sharing.

Free: automatic recording for 5 trial rides, then up to 4 manual recordings per day, with ads. Pro is a one-time purchase with no subscription that removes camera recording limits and ads.

The Wear OS companion shows speed and recording status and sends controls through your paired Android phone. The phone app must be running and connected to the camera.

Features vary by camera model and firmware. GPS accuracy varies; estimated lean angle is for reference. Set up while safely stationary and do not operate your phone while riding.
