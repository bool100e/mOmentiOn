Screen Guide

123456789101112
  1. 1Camera Logo

    The selected camera logo is displayed. Tap while connecting to switch brands.

  2. 2GPS & Speed Monitoring

    Shows GPS reception. Tap to turn speed monitoring on or off while keeping the camera connected.

  3. 3Live Activity Log

    Connection and recording events are logged in English, newest first.

  4. 4Floating Button

    Use the floating button to control the app.

  5. 5Main Speed Button

    Green indicates the camera is connected and ready to record; red indicates recording in progress. Manual recording is also supported.

  6. 6Automatic Recording Speeds

    The number beside the red dot is the configured recording start speed; the number beside the green dot is the configured recording stop speed.

  7. 7Phone Orientation

    The yellow dot follows the phone’s gravity direction.

  8. 8Connect, Reconnect & Cancel

    Start or retry a connection. Tap again while a connection task is in progress to cancel.

  9. 9Exit The App

    Stops all services and exits the app immediately.

  10. 10Settings

    Configure camera connections, recording thresholds, and app behavior.

  11. 11Saved Rides

    Review, compare, and share saved rides.

  12. 12Saved Logs

    Open saved logs to inspect past activity.
